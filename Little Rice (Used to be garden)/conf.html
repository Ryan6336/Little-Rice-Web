FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How much sunlight do plants need?

All plants need varying amounts of sunlight but 6+ hours in the sun should be optimal.

How do I test soil pH?

Mixing vinegar or baking soda with your soil will tell you your soil's pH, if its fizzing when mixed with baking soda its pH is below 7 and vice versa.

What are good starter plants?

Herbs such as basil, mint, and hardy flowers such as marigolds are easier choices.
