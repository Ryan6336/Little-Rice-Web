Gardening 101 — Background

Gardening is the practice of growing and cultivating plants. This page explains soil, fertilizers, watering and etc.

Soil basics

Soil provides structure, nutrients and a home for roots. Maintain your soil quality and your plants will thrive.

Do not over expose your plants

Although sunlight is essential for plant growth, it is recommended that you dont leave them in it for too long.

Water and drainage

Roots need oxygen as well as water. Avoid flooding the soil when watering your plants.
