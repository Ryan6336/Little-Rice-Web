ABOUT US

Growing up, we were lucky to have plenty of delicious, nourishing home-cooked meals around the table, It was the kind of food that was simple, comforting, and made with love

Little Rice House started from the same love for good food. We wanted to create takeaway meals that are not only yummy and convenient, but also feel a little more like a proper home-cooked meal.

Inspired by Taiwanese bento (便當 bian dang), Little Rice House is a home-based family business that provides a convenient way of eating a proper home-cooked meal. The aim is to provide a convenient way to enjoy a freshly packed bento made with care just like a meal from home.
