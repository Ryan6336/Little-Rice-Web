Deep Dive

Optimal times to water plants.

  • Watering plants in the afternoon may actually cause more harm then good, with there being two main reasons.
  • One being that water evaporates fast in the heat leading to most of the water evaporating before reaching the roots.
  • Another is that any water droplets left on the plants leaves can magnify the sunlight leading to it scalding the leaves.

Fertilizers 101.

  • Working with fertilizers is very akin to chemistry, due to reasons such as;
  • You must test your soil first as you need to find the right balance of many variable such as pH, nitrogen and etc.
  • Choosing the correct type of fertilizer, I would highly recomend using organic fertilizer such as chicken dropping infused soils as they build up great long term soil health.
  • Meanwhile the chemical fertilizers are also good in that they are fast acting, long term use of them can damage your plants.

The dangers of Pests and Weeds

  • Pests and weeds are the bane to most gardens for a many list of reasons such as;
  • Leaf eating pests can cripple or even kill garden plants by eating nutrient rich parts of the plants.
  • Some pests can infect your plants with diseases making the plant and perhaps adjecent plants sick, which can potentialy kill them.
  • Weeds although not as dangerous as pests still can stunt plant growth due to them stealing nutrients from the soil.
